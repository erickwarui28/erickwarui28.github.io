WWJD REFLECTIONS

Delete your account

You can delete your WWJD Reflections account here or in the app under Menu → Account & sync → Delete account.

Deletion permanently removes your sign-in account, synced answers, chats, notes and support records. Unused tokens are forfeited. It cannot be undone. Private backups expire within 30 days. Hashed identity, device and purchase records are retained only to prevent repeated credits. Google Play retains its own transaction records.

If a deletion is interrupted, retry on this page or in the app. App data already erased will not be restored. If you cannot sign in, request deletion help by email from your account address.

Confirm permanent deletion

Your account data and unused tokens will be removed permanently.
