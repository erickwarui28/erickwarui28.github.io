Structured & Thoughtful

What a Response Looks Like

Every "Ask for Wisdom" answer is organized to help you understand not just what to do, but why — with real Bible verses to explore further.

Your question: "I'm struggling to forgive someone who deeply hurt me. What would Jesus do?"

Short Answer

Jesus would encourage you to forgive — not because the hurt doesn't matter, but because forgiveness frees your heart and reflects God's mercy toward you.

Explanation

Jesus taught forgiveness repeatedly, even from the cross. Forgiveness is a process that honors your pain while releasing bitterness.

Bible References

  • Matthew 6:14–15
  • Colossians 3:13
  • Luke 23:34

Strengths

Inner peace, freedom from bitterness, alignment with Christ's example, and restored relationships.

Challenges

Forgiveness takes time. It doesn't mean forgetting or excusing harm — it means choosing not to be bound by it.
