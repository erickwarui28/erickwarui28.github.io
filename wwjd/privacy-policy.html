1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when using our App:

  • User Queries and Conversations: When you ask questions or seek guidance through the App, we collect the text or voice input you provide. This includes personal dilemmas, moral questions, and any other content you submit.
  • Voice Recordings: If you use the voice input feature, we temporarily process your voice recordings to convert speech to text. These recordings are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored by us.
  • Saved Conversations: If you choose to save conversations, we store them locally on your device using AsyncStorage.
  • Personal Notes and Reflections: Any notes or reflections you add to saved conversations are stored locally on your device.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use our App, we may automatically collect certain information:

  • Device Information: We may collect device identifiers (such as device ID) to provide personalized services and manage your account if you use Firebase features.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the App, including features used, frequency of use, and token usage statistics (if applicable).
  • Purchase Information: If you make in-app purchases, we collect transaction information, including purchase history and token balances.

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

We may receive information from third-party services integrated into our App:

  • Google Gemini API: When you submit queries, they are processed by Google's Gemini API. Google may collect and process this information according to their privacy policy.
  • Firebase: If you use Firebase features, Google Firebase may collect device and usage information according to their privacy policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To Provide Services: To process your queries, generate AI-powered responses, and deliver biblical guidance and wisdom.
  • To Improve Our Services: To analyze usage patterns, improve our AI responses, and enhance user experience.
  • To Manage Your Account: To maintain your saved conversations, preferences, and token balances (if applicable).
  • To Process Transactions: To handle in-app purchases and manage token systems.
  • To Communicate: To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To Ensure Security: To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and other harmful activities.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

3.1 Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:

  • Google Gemini API: Your queries are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate responses. Google processes this data according to their privacy policy.
  • Firebase (Google): If you use Firebase features, your data may be stored and processed by Google Firebase according to their privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing: Purchase transactions are processed by Google Play according to their terms and privacy policy.

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

3.3 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Important: We do not share your personal conversations or queries with other users or third parties for marketing purposes. Your spiritual and personal questions remain private.

4. Third-Party Services

Our App uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

4.1 Google Gemini API

We use Google's Gemini API to process your queries and generate AI responses. When you submit a query, it is sent to Google's servers for processing. Please review Google's Privacy Policy to understand how they handle your data.

4.2 Firebase (Google)

If you use Firebase features (cloud storage, authentication), your data may be stored on Google's Firebase servers. Please review Firebase Privacy Information for details.

4.3 Google Play Services

Our App uses Google Play Services for in-app purchases and other functionality. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for information about how Google handles your data.

Note: We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Local Storage

Much of your data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage:

  • Saved conversations and notes
  • App preferences and settings
  • Daily wisdom cache

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless you use Firebase cloud features.

5.2 Cloud Storage (Optional)

If you use Firebase features, some data may be stored in the cloud:

  • Device ID and user profile
  • Token balances and usage history
  • Purchase records

5.3 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS)
  • Secure local storage on your device
  • Firebase security rules to protect cloud data
  • Regular security assessments

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

6.1 Local Data

Data stored locally on your device (via AsyncStorage) remains on your device until you:

  • Delete the App
  • Clear the App's data through your device settings
  • Manually delete conversations within the App

6.2 Cloud Data

If you use Firebase features, we retain your data for as long as necessary to provide our services. You can request deletion of your cloud data by contacting us (see Contact Us section).

6.3 Third-Party Data

Data processed by third-party services (Google Gemini API, Firebase) is retained according to their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

7.1 Access and Portability

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you. You can view your saved conversations directly in the App.

7.2 Deletion

You can delete your saved conversations at any time through the App. To delete cloud data, please contact us.

7.3 Correction

You can edit or update your saved conversations and notes directly in the App.

7.4 Opt-Out

You can stop using the App at any time. You can also clear all App data through your device settings.

7.5 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right to request deletion of your personal information.

7.6 European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, and data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided in the Contact Us section.

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information from our systems.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
