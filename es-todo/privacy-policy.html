2. What the App does

OverPowered To-Do App helps you plan daily tasks, track weekly completions, organize projects/categories, save notes, attach optional images, and set local reminder notifications. You can sign in with Google, email/password, or username, and optionally enable Google Authenticator–style 2FA.

3. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Account information — email address, username, display name, authentication identifiers, and (if you choose Google Sign-In) basic Google profile details provided by Google for sign-in
  • Security data — password authentication credentials managed by Firebase Authentication; optional Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) / authenticator settings you enable
  • App content you create — tasks, due dates, completion status, notes, project/category names, reminder times, soft-delete/trash metadata, and optional images you attach
  • Device permissions (Android) — camera and/or photo library access only when you choose to attach an image; notification permission when you enable reminders
  • Technical/operational data — standard Firebase/Google service logs needed to authenticate, sync, and operate the App (for example, authentication events)

We do not sell personal information and do not use your task/note content for advertising or third-party marketing.

4. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and secure your account, including optional two-factor authentication
  • Store and sync your tasks, notes, projects, and images across your signed-in sessions
  • Schedule and show local reminder notifications you configure
  • Provide account features such as Trash (soft delete), restore, and permanent delete
  • Operate admin/support features that are limited to authorized administrators of the App
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve reliability of the service

5. Where your data is stored

Account and app content are stored using Google Firebase services (including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Optional task/note images are compressed and stored with your documents (as data URLs) rather than in a separate public media CDN.

On Android, reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Camera/gallery access is used only to pick or capture images you choose to attach.

Access to user content in Firestore is restricted by security rules so that, under normal operation, each signed-in user can only read and write their own data (except for limited admin tooling for authorized administrators).

6. Third-party services

The App relies on these providers to function:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — authentication and cloud database hosting
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method
  • Google Play — Android distribution and related Play services
  • GitHub Pages — hosting of the web app and this privacy policy page

Those providers process data according to their own privacy policies. We configure Firebase so your app content is associated with your account and not shared publicly by us.

7. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We share data only with the infrastructure providers listed above as needed to run the App, or if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect our rights, users, or the public where permitted by law.

8. Retention and deletion

  • While your account is active: we retain account and content data needed to provide the App.
  • Trash: items you soft-delete remain until you restore them or permanently delete them (or clear trash).
  • Account deletion / content deletion: you may permanently delete individual items in the App. To request full account deletion or help removing remaining data, email contact@ericksolutions.co.ke.
  • Uninstalling the Android app removes the local app and local notification schedules from that device, but does not by itself delete your cloud account data.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Enable or disable notifications and revoke camera/photos permission in Android settings
  • Enable, disable, or change authenticator 2FA from Account settings

Depending on your location (including the EEA, UK, Kenya, and other regions with data protection laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, or to data portability. Contact us to exercise these rights.
