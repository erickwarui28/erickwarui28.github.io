Privacy Policy

App: OverPowered To-Do App
Package: com.overpoweredtodo.app
Web app: erickwarui28.github.io/es-todo
Effective date: August 26, 2026
Last updated: November 17, 2026

OverPowered To-Do App uses Firebase for sign-in and a private production server for workspace data, account-linked support and subscription status. Pro subscriptions use Google Play Billing directly. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

1. Developer and contact

Erick Warui operates OverPowered To-Do App for Android and web. For privacy questions, access requests or deletion assistance, email erickwarui28@gmail.com. You can also use Contact admin while signed in.

2. Information processed

  • Account details: email, username, display name, authentication identifiers and basic Google profile details if you choose Google Sign-In. Firebase Authentication manages sign-in credentials. Optional authenticator settings help secure your app account.
  • Workspace content: tasks, due dates, recurrence and reminder settings, timer and completion history, projects, notes, optional attachments and Trash metadata.
  • Support: messages you send to administrators, their replies, read/unread state and the account identity needed to assist you.
  • Subscription information: product, expiry, renewal state, encrypted purchase tokens and account association. Google Play receives a hashed app account identifier. We do not receive payment card details.
  • Diagnostics and operational logs: authentication and app events, errors and device/app details for security and reliability. When administrator-enabled Sentry diagnostics are active, errors may include account identifiers and email. We do not use session replay or advertising identifiers.

3. Use and storage

We use this information to authenticate accounts, sync workspace content, deliver configured reminders, fulfil subscriptions, answer support requests, prevent abuse and troubleshoot failures.

Firebase Authentication provides identity. Our production VPS hosted by Contabo stores workspace records, app account settings, private attachments, subscription status and support conversations. Server authorization restricts content to its account and authorized administrator functions. Support messages are available to you and authorized administrators. Encrypted operational backups protect server records.

Your device retains cached content and pending edits for offline work. On Android, notifications are scheduled locally. The current app waits for sync and checks current server state before showing task, inactivity or motivation notifications; notifications wait while offline. The background-check copy of your server session is encrypted with Android Keystore. Camera/gallery access is used only for attachments you choose; notification permission is used for reminders.

4. Google Play billing

Optional monthly and yearly OP To-Do Pro subscriptions use Google Play Billing. Our server verifies purchases, acknowledgement, renewals, cancellations and refunds directly with Google Play and shares your account entitlement between Android and web. RevenueCat is no longer used by the current app. Eligible app trials or administrator grants do not create a Play charge. The web app does not process card payments.

Google handles payments and refunds under its own terms. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play. Canceling a subscription does not delete your app account; deleting your app account or uninstalling the app does not cancel a Play subscription. Cancel the subscription separately in Google Play.

5. Service providers and disclosure

  • Google Firebase/Google Cloud: authentication, identity configuration and billing-notification delivery.
  • Google Sign-In and Google Play: optional sign-in, Android distribution and billing.
  • Contabo: production server and private storage.
  • Sentry: error diagnostics when enabled by the administrator.
  • GitHub Pages: web app and privacy policy hosting.

Providers process data to deliver these services under their own policies. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service. Providers may process data outside your country under applicable safeguards.

6. Retention, deletion and choices

We retain account, workspace, support and entitlement records needed to provide the service. Trash items remain recoverable until you restore or permanently delete them. Use Account → Delete account to remove your app account and its workspace, support and stored purchase records, or email the developer for assistance. Encrypted operational backups may retain deleted records until they are manually retired; no automatic backup expiration period is currently configured. Google retains its own payment records under its policies.

You can edit or delete content, export your workspace, manage notifications and permissions, choose optional 2FA and manage subscriptions in Google Play. Uninstalling removes the local app and notification schedules but does not delete server data. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing and request portability. Contact the developer to exercise these rights.

7. Security and children

We use Firebase Authentication, server account authorization, HTTPS, encrypted sensitive tokens and backups, and optional 2FA. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your devices and use 2FA when available.

The app is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age in your country. We do not knowingly collect children's personal data. Contact us if a child has provided information.

8. Changes and contact

We update this policy when practices change and revise the date above. Material updates may also be communicated in the app or listing. Contact erickwarui28@gmail.com for questions.
