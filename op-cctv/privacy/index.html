OP CCTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

OP CCTV (“the app”) lets you use one Android phone as a camera and another as a viewer over the same Wi‑Fi network or, optionally, over the internet.

Who we are

OP CCTV is published by OverPowered. Contact: erickwarui28@gmail.com.

What the app does

  • LAN / Wi‑Fi mode: Live video and audio stay on your local network. No account is required.
  • Internet / remote mode: Optional. Uses an invitation, encrypted signaling, and TURN relay so a viewer can watch a camera from another network.

Data we process

  • Camera and microphone media: Captured on your devices for live viewing and optional local recordings you choose to keep. We do not store your live streams on our servers.
  • Viewer install id: An opaque id used to manage remote-access entitlement (trial or Play subscription).
  • Purchase tokens: When you subscribe to remote access, Google Play purchase tokens may be verified on our server so entitlement stays accurate.
  • Optional support messages: If you use in-app support chat, message text and optional contact email you provide are stored so we can reply.
  • Device labels / models: May be attached to support threads you open, to help troubleshooting.

Where processing happens

Remote signaling and entitlement run on infrastructure we operate (currently a VPS at 67.211.211.236 under the /op-cctv/ path). Media between phones is exchanged peer-to-peer when possible, or via TURN when needed for connectivity.

Device-side detection

Motion and human detection run on the camera phone. Snapshot alerts are sent to approved viewers over the encrypted device connection. Local event history and camera clips are managed on your devices.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use your camera or microphone for advertising.
  • We do not require an account for LAN viewing.

Subscriptions and cancellation

Remote internet viewing may require a Google Play subscription or a limited server-managed trial. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions).

Permissions

The app may request camera, microphone, notifications, and related foreground-service permissions so a camera phone can keep streaming while the screen is off, and so a viewer can receive alerts.

Retention

Entitlement and support records are kept only as long as needed to provide the service and respond to requests. Camera recordings are managed within the storage budget selected in the app; the oldest completed clips may be deleted automatically. Viewer recordings and screenshots stay on your device until you delete them.

Your choices

  • Use LAN mode only and never enable internet / remote access.
  • Revoke camera or microphone permission in Android settings.
  • Email us to ask about deletion of server-side viewer or support records tied to your install id.

Children

OP CCTV is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
