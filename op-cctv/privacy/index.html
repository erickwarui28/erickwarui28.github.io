OP CCTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

OP CCTV (“the app”) lets you use one Android phone as a camera and another as a viewer over the same Wi‑Fi network or, optionally, over the internet.

Who we are

OP CCTV is published by OverPowered. Contact: erickwarui28@gmail.com.

What the app does

  • Same Wi‑Fi: Live video and audio stay on your Wi‑Fi network. No account is required.
  • Watching over the internet: An invitation lets a viewer watch from another network. We protect the information your devices exchange to connect.

Data we process

  • Camera video and microphone audio: Your devices capture video and audio for live viewing and for recordings you choose to keep on your phone. We do not store your live streams on our servers.
  • Pro cloud human events: On Pro, the camera uploads new human detection photos to cloud storage. You can turn uploads off in Camera Settings.
  • App installation code: We use a code assigned to your copy of the app to keep track of your trial or subscription.
  • Google Play purchase information: We may use the purchase reference from Google Play to check whether your subscription is active.
  • Optional support messages: If you use in-app support chat, message text and optional contact email you provide are stored so we can reply.
  • Device labels / models: May be attached to support threads you open, to help troubleshooting.

How your devices connect

OP CCTV uses servers to connect your devices over the internet and check remote-access subscriptions and trials. Live video and audio travel directly between your devices when possible and through our servers when needed to connect. Pro cloud photos are stored on our servers as described above.

Motion and human detection

The camera phone detects motion and people. It sends alert photos to approved viewers over a protected connection. The camera keeps its own event history and recordings on the phone, and Pro can also save human detection photos in the cloud.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use your camera or microphone for advertising.
  • We do not require an account to watch on the same Wi‑Fi.

Subscriptions and cancellation

Watching over the internet may require a Google Play subscription or a limited free trial. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions).

Permissions

The app may ask to use the camera and microphone, show notifications, and keep running while the screen is off. This lets the camera keep streaming and the viewer receive alerts.

How long we keep your data

We keep trial, subscription, and support records only as long as we need them to provide the service and respond to requests. Camera recordings and photos stay on the camera phone until you delete them or the app removes older items to stay within your storage limit. Recordings and screenshots you save on a viewer phone stay there until you delete them. We remove cloud photos when we fulfill a deletion request or reach your cloud storage limit. We remove the oldest photos first. New uploads stop when Pro ends.

Your choices

  • Use the app on the same Wi‑Fi without turning on internet access.
  • Turn off camera or microphone access in Android settings.
  • Stop cloud uploads in the camera’s Storage settings.
  • Email us to ask us to delete cloud photos or viewer and support records we store that are tied to your install.

Children

OP CCTV is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
