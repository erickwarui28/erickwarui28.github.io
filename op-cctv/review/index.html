APP REVIEW · 11 NOVEMBER 2026

Foreground-service demonstration

OP CCTV 1.1.0 (versionCode 5), using a physical TECNO LE6 camera and a BlueStacks viewer. These are recordings of the running app, edited to remove idle time. No camera scenes, app controls or notifications were synthesized.

Camera and microphone

The camera phone starts capture through Start camera. The recording shows the live camera and the explicit Mic off → Mic on control, followed by backgrounding and returning to the camera. Camera capture and optional microphone input support live viewing and local camera recordings. A persistent camera notification offers a Stop camera action.

Open camera/microphone video directly

This is a silent screen recording of the permission-dependent UI flow, not an audio sample. Capture begins only after a user-facing start action. The microphone is optional.

Connection to the camera device

The approved viewer maintains an active connection to the external camera phone for live media and alerts. Watch for alerts in the background enables background monitoring. The ongoing OverPowered CCTV monitoring alerts notification is visible outside the viewer and returns the user to the active connection; its expanded notification includes Stop monitoring.

Open connected-device video directly

The clip starts with the actual live viewer. Its final section is a clearly labeled still capture of the actual ongoing foreground notification. It is cropped to exclude unrelated account/system notifications. This supplement is used because the emulator screen recorder did not reliably capture its notification shade.

All demonstration camera, microphone and viewer monitoring sessions were stopped after capture. Services do not automatically resume after force-stop, process death or reboot; the user must start them again.
